Key Updates & Points to Note

  • Tax Audit Report (s.44AB) for AY 2026-27 – CBDT has extended the due date from 30 Sep to 21 Oct 2026; ITR for such cases extended from 31 Oct to 21 Nov 2026 (CBDT press release dated 28 Sep 2026; formal notification to follow).
  • New Income-tax Act, 2025 is effective from 1 Apr 2026 – TDS/TCS statements now use new forms (Form 138 / 140 / 144 / 143); AY 2026-27 returns continue under the 1961 Act.
  • Weekend / holiday dates – GST dates falling on Sat/Sun and the 20 Oct (Dussehra) holiday are not automatically extended; file early.
  • GSTR-3B (QRMP) – Gujarat falls in Category X: due 22 Oct.
